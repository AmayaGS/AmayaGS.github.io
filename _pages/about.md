I'm a machine learning researcher and computational biologist working at the intersection of 💻 **AI** and 🧬 **biology**. I build explainable, biology-aligned models and probe what foundation models actually encode about biological structure — across single-cell genomics and computational pathology, in immunology and cancer.

I'm currently a **Postdoctoral Research Fellow** jointly at [Queen Mary University of London](https://www.qmul.ac.uk/) and [Imperial College London](https://www.imperial.ac.uk/), supported by a Wellcome Trust Early Career Transition award. In **September 2027** I'll join Imperial's [I-X](https://www.imperial.ac.uk/i-x/) as an **[Eric and Wendy Schmidt AI for Science Fellow](https://www.schmidtsciences.org/)**, where I'll lead an independent research programme on identifiable representation learning and cross-modal causal discovery for biology.

I completed my [Wellcome Trust](https://wellcome.org/) PhD at Queen Mary in 2025, advised by Professors [Michael Barnes](https://www.qmul.ac.uk/whri/people/academic-staff/items/barnesmichael.html), [Myles Lewis](https://www.qmul.ac.uk/whri/people/academic-staff/items/lewismyles.html) and [Greg Slabaugh](https://www.eecs.qmul.ac.uk/~gslabaugh/), and hold an MSc in Mathematical Sciences (Distinction) and a Licentiate in Computational Biology from the Universidad de Buenos Aires.

### Research interests

- **Explainable, biology-aligned deep learning** — graph and attention-based models for multi-stain, multimodal computational pathology
- **Probing biological foundation models** — what single-cell and pathology foundation models encode about regulatory and tissue structure
- **Identifiable representation learning & causal discovery** — cross-modal, interventional and dynamical models
- **Toward mechanistic virtual-cell models** — connecting genomics, imaging and perturbation data

I care deeply about work that helps us understand the complex, wonderful world of biology, immunology and pathogenesis ✨ — and about doing it openly and rigorously. If any of this resonates, feel free to [reach out](mailto:a.gallaghersyed@gmail.com).
